OAuth Callback

This page is the registered redirect URI for TikTok Login Kit authorization responses for the private Channel13 Auto-Poster app.

When the operator authorizes the app on their own TikTok account (@channel13ai), TikTok redirects here with an authorization code, which the app exchanges for publishing tokens. That exchange happens server-side; this page itself collects nothing and stores nothing.

Not a public login page. Channel13 Auto-Poster has exactly one user — its operator — and offers no public sign-up or sign-in. If you arrived here without authorizing the app, there is nothing further to do.
